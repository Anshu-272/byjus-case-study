
# BYJU'S Business Case Study 📊

## Project Overview
This project analyzes BYJU'S business journey, rapid growth, aggressive acquisitions, funding, financial losses, and competitive challenges. The goal is to understand the key factors behind the company's financial crisis using data analysis and visualization.

## Business Problem
BYJU'S experienced significant growth and became one of India's leading EdTech companies. However, aggressive expansion, high spending, acquisitions, and increasing financial pressure raised concerns about the sustainability of its business model.

This case study explores the company's growth, financial performance, and the warning signs behind its collapse.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Power BI
- Data Analysis and Data Visualization

## Key Areas of Analysis
- Revenue and financial losses
- Funding and company valuation
- Employee and user growth
- Paid subscriber trends
- Acquisitions and acquisition costs
- Marketing expenditure
- Competitor and market share comparison
- Business risks and recommendations

## Key Findings
- BYJU'S experienced rapid user and employee growth.
- The company raised substantial funding and achieved a high valuation.
- Aggressive acquisitions increased business complexity and financial pressure.
- Financial losses and spending raised concerns about sustainable growth.
- Competition from other EdTech companies intensified.
- Sustainable profitability and better financial management are important for long-term business success.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on sustainable and profitable growth.
- Monitor marketing expenditure and return on investment (ROI).
- Evaluate acquisitions carefully before investing.
- Improve cost control and financial transparency.
- Reduce excessive dependence on external funding.
- Strengthen business governance and financial planning.

## Conclusion
This case study highlights the importance of balancing business growth with profitability, responsible spending, effective acquisitions, and sound financial management.

**Key takeaway:** Valuation creates headlines, but profitability creates longevity.
